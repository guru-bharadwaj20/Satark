# Disclaimer

**Satark gives a risk assessment, not a guarantee.** It can be wrong in both directions: it can miss a scam, and it can flag something harmless.

- A result that shows no warning signs does **not** mean something is safe. If anything feels off, contact the organisation through its official app, website or the number printed on your card.
- Banks, the RBI, police, courier companies and government bodies never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card CVV or password, and never ask you to install a screen-sharing app.
- Community reports show how many independent people reported a number, UPI ID or website. They are not a finding of fraud and are not a statement about any person.

## If you've lost money

1. Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately. Speed matters: the helpline can ask banks to hold the money.
2. File a complaint at **https://cybercrime.gov.in** and keep the acknowledgement number.
3. Call your bank on its official number to block cards, UPI or net banking.
4. Report the fraudulent call, SMS or WhatsApp message on **Chakshu** at **https://sancharsaathi.gov.in**.
