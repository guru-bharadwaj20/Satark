Privacy and disclaimer

What happens to what you check

Messages, links and QR images you check are not stored after the check. Phone numbers, UPI IDs, account numbers and OTPs are removed before any text is sent to an AI model. The web app keeps nothing in your browser except the app itself, so it works offline.

What we never do

  • Sell data, show ads or build profiles.
  • Ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, password or bank details.
  • Fill in forms or enter data on the links we inspect.
  • Call any person a scammer.

Your rights

If you use the Telegram bot, send /forget to delete your settings, history and reports. For anything else, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub page.

Disclaimer

Satark gives a risk assessment, not a guarantee. It can miss a scam or flag something harmless. A result with no warning signs does not mean something is safe. Satark is an independent open-source project, not a government service.

Lost money? Call 1930 immediately, report at cybercrime.gov.in, and report fraud calls and messages on Chakshu at sancharsaathi.gov.in.

The full policy is in the project repository under docs/privacy-policy.md.
